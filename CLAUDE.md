### What o3 deducted from my style
| Dimension           | What to tell the LLM                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tone**            | Friendly, blunt, occasionally cheeky. Mix first-person “I” with inclusive “we.” Never overly polished; let a bit of swagger show through.                               |
| **Formality**       | Informal business. Contractions, plain words, and the odd slangy aside (“hottie,” “sweaty guy”) are OK.                                                                 |
| **Rhythm**          | Short paragraphs (1-3 lines). Frequently break flow with one-line statements or questions for punch.                                                                    |
| **Structure**       | • Clear H2/H3 headings.<br>• Ordered or bulleted lists for facts and steps.<br>• Quotations and parenthetical side notes.                                               |
| **Content mix**     | Blend hard numbers or facts (revenues, %. time saved) with anecdotes and mini-stories.                                                                                  |
| **Typical devices** | - Rhetorical questions (“How come?”).<br>- Self-deprecating humor.<br>- Metaphors drawn from everyday life or travel.<br>- Direct calls to the reader (“Here’s how …”). |
| **Voice markers**   | Uses “period.” to end emphatic statements; calls out industry clichés (“thermal printers,” “VIP package”) and then debunks them.                                        |

- Punchy openings (start with the verdict, not a buildup).
