# Bootstrap Everything

I started making banner ads for $100 a month. Used cracked Photoshop because the licensed copy cost half my annual salary.

So I know what it's like to be broke.

This book isn't about raising millions or scaling to unicorn status. It's about doing more with less. About finding pleasure in cutting waste. About turning constraints into advantages.

Here's what I learned building companies from nothing.

## Management

### The Developer Who Worked Two Jobs

I was looking at the code for our chairlift automation project. That was great, except we didn't have a chairlift. My teammate's ex-employer had.

That's how I learned our employee was working his previous job while billing us. I don't even think he left the previous job. 

The solution: taking screenshots. We wouldn't exist without that.

### Control freak vs. wimp

Both extremes are bad, but a wimpy manager is also pathetic. He is like an uncool kid trying to make friends with cool kids. 

How to know when there's too much or too little control? By listening people:
- When there's too little control, people ask: "What are we doing? What's next? What are we doing as a company?"
- When there's too much control, people say: "I have tons of meetings. I want to work at some point."

The sweet spot? When you speak about what to do, not how to do it.

### How not to be an asshole

If you don't want to be an asshole, use Scrum. If you follow industry standards, you're not asshole. People might dislike Scrum, but they can't blame you for its existence.

The beauty of Scrum is it's like a gas pedal; you can press it as hard as you want:
- Need more structure? Follow Scrum precisely. 
    - Scrum poker for the tiniest issues
    - Retrospectives
    - Acceptance criteria every day.
- Need more freedom? Skip some meetings
    - Retrospective merged with some other meeting
    - Easy on velocity
    - 0.5 story points for small tasks. Say "I put 0.5, objections?"

## Gaining Authority among Developers

Being a Scrum Master is easy. I did a decent job without any prior experience.

It's common wisdon that Scrum Masters should be experienced developers. I was not. I sucked so much at management that I didn't even know JSON existed. I told developers to use XML. Only realized it was stupid after both backend and frontend wrote converters from JSON to XML and vice versa.

But I had authority because I'd read two books about Scrum, with a seal on the cover and with an elephant. I also asked others to read it. As nobody elese had read it, I was the only one who knew how to do Scrum. Boom! Scrum Master.

### Culture Levels

There are several levels:
1. My life sucks
2. I'm great, you're not  
3. We are great
4. We're changing the world

Fire people from level one. They're toxic.

Hire for level three or four.

## Marketing

### The Freemium Trap That Actually Works

If you create a paid product nobody uses, that's a fail. If you create a free product everybody uses and it goes viral, you win. You can add paywalls wherever you want later.

It's a pleasure to deal with this problem.

### The Dark Side Goes Viral

Want a viral product? Think about the evil side, not just the good side. The good side is 100 times harder.

When we created 100,000 Faces, it went viral. I got interviewed by Washington Post and BBC. They all had the same story in mind: "How horrible is this and how do we save our families from it?"

That's how you get attention.

### SEO for Poor People

Whatever you do marketing-wise, it's easier to convert into backlinks than traffic. And backlinks convert to traffic.

Do a little SEO. Whatever viral article you have gets backlinked from 150 domains. That becomes Google traffic later.

### ChatGPT SEO is Different

ChatGPT SEO is easier and harder than regular SEO.

Easier: you don't need thousands of backlinks. Several quality ones work.

Harder: you need backlinks from pages ChatGPT considers authorities. You need mentions in exactly the articles ChatGPT uses as inspiration.

Getting the list is easy. Just ask: "Give me your sources."

## Finance

### Kill the Budget

Budgets are lies about optimal pricing. Instead of guessing numbers, find the real market price.

When we were doing photo shoots, our goal was three scenes: office, home, and pajamas. We went to Zara and bought the clothes. No budget would've predicted that cost.

Shop for actual prices. Cherry-pick offers. Don't inflate numbers to feel safe.

### Always Shop for Prices

Always. Every single purchase. Get pleasure from finding better deals.

If you find waste, remove it immediately. Don't wait. Don't plan. Cut it now.
